Boomerang Tasks

A to-do list for forgetful people. It keeps reminding you until it's done, without judgement.

Common questions

Why does the app need access to Reminders?

Your tasks are stored in Apple Reminders, so they stay on your devices and sync with iCloud without an account. If you said no, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Reminders → Boomerang.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are on in Settings → Notifications → Boomerang, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. Opening the app now and then also tops up its scheduled reminders.

How do I stop a task reminding me?

Mark it done, snooze it, or move it to another day. Swipe the task, long-press it, or use the buttons on the notification.

Some of my lists are missing.

Lists can be hidden in the app's Settings. Make sure the list is turned on there.

Location reminders don't nag like other tasks.

Arriving and leaving alerts are delivered by Apple Reminders, so they come once rather than repeating until done.
