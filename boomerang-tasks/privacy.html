Privacy Policy

Boomerang Tasks · Effective 26 October 2026

In short: Boomerang Tasks doesn't collect, store or share your personal data. Your tasks live in Apple Reminders on your devices and in your own iCloud account. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Your tasks

Boomerang Tasks reads and writes your tasks in Apple Reminders, with your permission. They're stored by Apple on your devices and, if you use it, in the account your Reminders sync with (such as iCloud). The developer has no access to them. You can turn off Reminders access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Reminders.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information on your device only:

  • Your settings, such as snooze lengths, which lists are hidden and the accent colour.
  • Which tasks you've snoozed recently, so it can offer longer snooze options.
  • "Repeat from completion date" rules, which Reminders can't store itself.
  • Places you've recently used for location reminders, and the names you gave them.

This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Dictation

If you dictate a task, the app uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into text. Where your device supports it, this happens entirely on the device. Otherwise Apple processes the audio under Apple's Privacy Policy. Audio isn't recorded or kept by the app, and it's never sent to the developer.

Location

Location is only used when you choose to attach a place to a task. Searching for a place uses Apple Maps. If you choose your current location, the app asks for permission and reads your location once. Reminders that fire when you arrive at or leave a place are handled by Apple Reminders and iOS, not by the app.

Notifications and Siri

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. If you add tasks with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple handles that request under its own privacy policy.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • No servers run by the developer. The app never sends your data to the developer.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
