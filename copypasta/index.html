Common questions

How do I save something?

Share it to CopyPasta from any app, use the paste button, or drag it into the app. On the Mac you can also press ⇧⌘V in the app, or ⌃⌥⌘S from anywhere to save what's on the clipboard.

My items aren't syncing between devices.

Make sure each device is signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud Sync is on in CopyPasta's Settings. If you just changed that setting, quit and reopen the app. New items usually arrive within a minute.

I deleted something by mistake.

Open Recently Deleted at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Restore. Deleted items stay there for 7 days, then they're removed from all your devices.

How do I use the Quick Picker on the Mac?

Press ⌃⌥⌘V from any app to search your library and copy an item without switching apps. You can turn the global shortcuts and the menu bar icon on or off in Settings.

Why won't a large file save?

Items can be up to 100 MB, so they stay quick to copy and sync.
