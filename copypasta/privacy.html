Privacy Policy

CopyPasta · Effective 5 November 2026

In short: CopyPasta doesn't collect, store or share your personal data. What you save stays on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud account. Nothing is sent to the developer.

What you save

Text, links, photos, GIFs and files you save are stored on your device. If iCloud Sync is on, they're also stored in your private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. Apple stores this data under Apple's Privacy Policy, and the developer has no access to it. You can turn off sync at any time in CopyPasta's Settings.

Your clipboard

CopyPasta only reads the clipboard when you ask it to, for example by pressing the paste button or Save Clipboard. It doesn't watch or record your clipboard in the background.

Links

When you save a link, CopyPasta fetches the page's title and preview image directly from that website so it can show a nice card. This works like opening the link in a browser: the website sees an ordinary request from your device. Nothing passes through the developer.

Deleted items

Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 7 days so you can restore them. After that they're permanently removed from your devices and your iCloud account.

Mac keyboard shortcuts

The global shortcuts only listen for CopyPasta's own key combinations. The app doesn't record or read anything else you type.

Shortcuts and Siri

If you use CopyPasta with Shortcuts or Siri, Apple handles those requests under its own privacy policy.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • No servers run by the developer. The app never sends your data to the developer.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
